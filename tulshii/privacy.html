Privacy at Tulshii

Your personal workspace

Tulshii is designed to keep personal workspace data on your device or under your control wherever practical. Features that require account access, syncing or collaboration use only the information required for those features.

Groups and shared spaces

Your personal workspace is separate from shared spaces. Joining a group does not automatically expose your private subjects, tasks, notes or other personal information. Information intentionally placed in a shared space may be visible to the members of that space.

Future AI features

Prem AI is designed around minimum necessary context so it can help answer a question without making the AI service the permanent home for your Tulshii history.
